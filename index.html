FAQ

Questions, answered

Can't find what you're looking for? Our team is one message away.

How long does shipping take?

Orders ship within 24 hours. Delivery usually takes 2–4 days in India and 5–9 days internationally, with tracking on every order.

What does the warranty cover?

Every Nova device includes a 2-year warranty covering manufacturing defects and battery degradation beyond 80% of original capacity.

Can I return a product?

Yes. You have 30 days to try your purchase. If you're not happy, we'll arrange a free pickup and a full refund.

Which payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI, all major credit and debit cards, net banking and cash on delivery on eligible orders.
